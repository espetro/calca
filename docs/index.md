# Calca

Calca is an open canvas for every format. Describe a page, a prototype, a brand
board, a deck slide — get live variations as movable frames, then remix, export,
or hand the board to your agent.

- **Open source** — every layer of the stack is open, end to end.
- **BYOK** — bring your own AI provider; your key never leaves your browser.
- **Web + desktop** — use the app at
  [calca.illo.fyi/app](https://calca.illo.fyi/app/) or download the desktop build.
- **Agent-ready** — the `calca` CLI exposes the canvas over MCP so Claude,
  Cursor, Codex and friends can read, propose, and arrange boards.
